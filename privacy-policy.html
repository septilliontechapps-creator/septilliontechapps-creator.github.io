Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026 · Applies to: Jewel Crush Kingdom and all apps published by Septillion Technology

This policy explains what information our apps collect, why, and the choices you have. Septillion Technology ("we", "us") is based in India.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None that identifies you. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run accounts or logins.

Your game progress, scores and settings (sound, music, language, skin) are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our apps are free and show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, if you allow it)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the app, you are asked whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in the app under Settings → More Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone: iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. More Games

The More Games section loads extra games from our website (this site, hosted by GitHub Pages) and needs an internet connection. When a game loads, the hosting provider receives standard technical data such as your IP address, as with any website. We do not use this to identify you.

4. Children

Our games are suitable for a general audience but are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our app, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the DPDP Act in India, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold personal data about you, most requests concern data processed by Google for ads. You can manage it through the choices above or at My Ad Center. For anything else, email us.

6. Security

We keep the data we handle to a minimum. Connections to our website and to Google's ad services are encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
